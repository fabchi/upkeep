# upkeep.

**Keep your Mac at its best.**

Update. Clean. Protect. Optimize.

## Why Upkeep?

Upkeep was born from a practical need we encountered in our everyday work as developers. Reclaiming disk space, removing unnecessary files, keeping software up to date and checking system security should be simple, fast and free.

Instead of relying on multiple tools or spending time running repetitive manual commands, we wanted to bring essential Mac maintenance tasks together in one native application.

## Our mission

Make Mac maintenance accessible to everyone.

- Reclaim disk space and identify unnecessary files, outdated caches and duplicates.
- Keep macOS, applications and development tools up to date, using their original update providers.
- Understand system health and security settings.
- Stay in control of every operation.

## Our principles

- **Privacy:** 100% local processing. Zero data collected by Upkeep.
- **Open-source & Free:** No subscriptions, paywalls or ads. Official releases are free.
- **Safety first:** Transparent previews, explicit approval and reversible operations wherever possible.
- **Simplicity:** Essential tools without unnecessary complexity.
- **Native macOS:** Swift and SwiftUI, with Intel and Apple Silicon as target platforms.

Local features require no cloud service. Explicit update checks may contact Apple or other official software providers, subject to their own privacy policies.

## Status

Upkeep is in early development. The features above describe our goals, not a released or validated product. Approved source code and releases will be published here under the MIT license.

**Free. Forever.**
